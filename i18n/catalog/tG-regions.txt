<p>Johann Heinrich von Thünen (1826) imagined an isolated city on a flat, even plain. Farmers pay to carry goods to market, so the <b>land rent</b> a crop can pay falls with distance:</p><p>$$R = Y(p - c) - Y f d$$</p><p>with $Y$ the yield per hectare, $p$ the market price, $c$ the production cost, $f$ the transport cost per tonne per km and $d$ the distance.
