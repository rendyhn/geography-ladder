<p>A population grows by <b>births</b> (B) and <b>immigration</b> (I) and shrinks by <b>deaths</b> (D) and <b>emigration</b> (E). Births minus deaths is <b>natural increase</b>; immigration minus emigration is <b>net migration</b>.</p>

<p><b>The Human Development Index</b> (HDI, <i>Indeks Pembangunan Manusia</i>) combines three dimensions. Each is turned into an index between 0 and 1:</p><p>$$\text{index} = \frac{\text{actual value} - \text{minimum}}{\text{maximum} - \text{minimum}}$$</p><p>For life expectancy the minimum is 20 years and the maximum 85. The HDI is the geometric mean of the three indices:</p><p>$$\text{HDI} = \sqrt[3]{I_{\text{health}} \times I_{\text{education}} \times I_{\text{income}}}$$</p>
